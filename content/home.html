Questions founders ask

Straight answers.

What is EM Growth Group (E.G.G.)?

EM Growth Group (E.G.G.) is a boutique, founder-led, AI-native revenue growth consultancy. It finds where growing businesses are losing revenue and builds the systems to fix it: digital marketing, social media, AI agents and automation, and revenue operations, run as one team under one P&L owner.

What is a revenue gap?

A revenue gap is the difference between what your business earns and what it should earn. It comes in two forms: leaks, money bleeding out through broken funnels, wasted ad spend, and manual processes, and gaps, revenue not yet captured in channels, offers, and systems you haven't built. The Revenue Gap Diagnostic finds both and prioritizes every fix by ROI.

Who does E.G.G. work with?

DTC, e-commerce, and tech founders between $100K and $5M+ in revenue: businesses with real traction and a growing team, but no CMO yet. If you have a gut feeling that revenue should be higher than it is, you're right, and we find the gap.

What services does E.G.G. provide?

Four pillars: digital marketing (paid media, SEO, GEO, email, conversion), social media marketing (content engines, short-form video, community), AI & automation (AI agents, n8n workflows, custom apps and dashboards, digital transformation), and revenue operations (process, data, and the funnel). You can engage through a diagnostic, a monthly retainer, or a full transformation.

What does it cost to work with a revenue growth consultancy?

In the US market in 2026, a marketing audit or growth diagnostic typically runs $5,000 to $15,000, fractional CMO retainers run $5,000 to $20,000 per month, and custom AI and automation builds range from $20,000 to $100,000. E.G.G. engagements typically come in below these ranges. Not because the work is lighter, but because you work directly with the founder and a lean remote specialist team instead of paying for agency overhead. Every engagement starts with the Revenue Gap Diagnostic, priced by ROI.

How is E.G.G. different from a digital marketing agency?

Agencies sell channels: SEO, ads, social. E.G.G. starts by diagnosing where revenue is actually leaking, then designs and deploys the fix across marketing, operations, and technology. Most consultants advise; E.G.G. operates. The track record: 116% revenue growth delivered, 98% client retention, $3M+ in P&L owned.

How fast will I see results?

The Revenue Diagnostic is delivered within weeks, and the 90-Day Revenue Blueprint pairs quick wins with long plays. Quick wins create momentum in the first 30 to 60 days; long plays build durable growth over the following quarters.

What brands has EM Growth Group worked with?

We have worked with startups and growing businesses in various industries such as retail, FMCG, tech, service, and e-commerce, spanning revenue leadership roles on both the agency and client side.
